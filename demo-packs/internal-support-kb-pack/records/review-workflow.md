# Review Workflow

## Summary

The fictional review workflow moves article gaps from intake to draft, human review, approval, and scheduled recheck.

## Stages

1. Capture the gap.
2. Draft an update.
3. Review for safety and accuracy.
4. Approve the record.
5. Set a future review date.
