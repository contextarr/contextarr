# Support Matrix

## Summary

The fictional matrix maps demo product areas to support paths and article categories.

## Matrix

| Area | Primary Article | Review Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Setup | Getting Started | Demo Support |
| Billing concept | Plan FAQ | Demo Operations |
| Troubleshooting | Known Issues | Demo Product |
| Compatibility | Requirements | Demo Support |

## Support Boundaries

| Topic | Safe support answer | Must avoid |
|---|---|---|
| Buyer fit | Match the fictional segment to Mini, Core, or Max and explain the tradeoff. | Real market claims or competitor comparisons. |
| Packaging | Describe invented tiers as demo positioning only. | Prices, discounts, contract terms, or availability dates. |
| Compatibility | Point to reviewed requirements and ask clarifying questions. | Guarantees about unlisted systems, vendors, or integrations. |
| Release notes | Summarize fictional changes as public-safe examples. | Roadmap promises, incident claims, or warranty language. |

If a buyer asks for a promise outside the reviewed material, the assistant should mark it as unsupported and offer a discovery question instead of stretching the product story.

## Notes

The matrix avoids real pricing, account, or customer information.
