# Product Research Kit Sample Export

## Kit Summary

Use this kit to compare product notes, known issues, and support positioning.

## Task Goal

Prepare a source-backed research summary without market claims or real customer data.

## Included Materials

Context Packs:
- fake-product-line-pack
- internal-support-kb-pack

Skills:
- research-synthesis-skill
- internal-kb-answering-skill

## Example Output Shape

1. Situation summary.
2. Relevant context.
3. Suggested response or implementation outline.
4. Review checklist.
5. Open questions.

## Guardrails

- Separate facts from recommendations.
- Keep demo product names fictional.
- Mark unknowns as follow-up questions.
