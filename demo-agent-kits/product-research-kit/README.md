# Product Research Kit

Public-safe kit for synthesizing fictional product-line notes into a research brief.

## Purpose

Use this kit to compare product notes, known issues, and support positioning.

## Intended Use

Prepare a source-backed research summary without market claims or real customer data.

## Included Context Packs

- fake-product-line-pack
- internal-support-kb-pack

## Included Skills

- research-synthesis-skill
- internal-kb-answering-skill

## Safety Notes

- Data is fake and public-safe.
- The kit is data-only and non-executable.
- Generated briefs are derived artifacts, not source of truth.
- Pack and Skill files stay unchanged during preview or export.
