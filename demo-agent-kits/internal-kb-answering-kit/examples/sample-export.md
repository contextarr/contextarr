# Internal KB Answering Kit Sample Export

## Kit Summary

Use this kit to produce grounded internal answers with citations to included demo sources.

## Task Goal

Prepare a safe answer that separates confirmed facts from follow-up needs.

## Included Materials

Context Packs:
- internal-support-kb-pack
- fake-product-line-pack

Skills:
- internal-kb-answering-skill
- research-synthesis-skill

## Example Output Shape

1. Situation summary.
2. Relevant context.
3. Suggested response or implementation outline.
4. Review checklist.
5. Open questions.

## Guardrails

- Keep answers short enough for support review.
- Flag missing source coverage.
- Do not invent customer or account details.
