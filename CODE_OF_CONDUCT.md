# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We want Contextarr to be a practical, respectful, and security-conscious project. Participants are expected to be constructive, patient, and clear.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful of different experience levels.
- Keep discussion focused on the work and the project boundaries.
- Give actionable feedback.
- Treat privacy and security concerns seriously.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information or credentials.
- Encouraging unsafe handling of secrets, private data, or user systems.
- Repeatedly pushing the project outside its stated safety boundaries after maintainers have clarified scope.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, or restrict participation when behavior is harmful to the project or its contributors.

Security issues should be reported to security@contextarr.com.
